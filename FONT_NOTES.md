Arvilo V45 typography update
- Replaced the app shell's VT323/Inter-heavy look with a Pinterest-inspired font system.
- Display/headlines/brand: Playfair Display.
- Main UI/body: Raleway.
- Buttons/navigation/forms: Rubik.
- Portfolio template previews remain isolated so each template keeps its own intended typography.
- Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts at runtime.
- Reference basis: Pinterest typography guidance and Pinterest's published font list, including Playfair Display and Raleway.
